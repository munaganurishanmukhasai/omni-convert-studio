Questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is processed inside your browser, so your files stay on your device.

Which formats can I open?

JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, SVG, PDF and camera RAW previews.

My file will not open. What should I do?

Tap the box and choose the file again, or drag it in or paste it. Very large files may need a smaller target size.
